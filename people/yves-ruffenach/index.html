Yves Ruffenach

Research Engineer · Founder of JWAYU · Strasbourg, France

Research profile

Yves Ruffenach develops the EVE variational neuron and studies how local probabilistic computation can make uncertainty measurable inside neural networks and language models. The research program covers the computational primitive, its operating regime, Transformer integration, reliability monitoring and uncertainty-aware control.
